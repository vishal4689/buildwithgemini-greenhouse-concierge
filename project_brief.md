# My agent: Greenhouse Concierge
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts care for and discover indoor plants with a catalog of houseplants.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Plant collection, watering and care history, lighting conditions at home
- Tools: Check care schedules, search plant catalog, diagnose plant symptoms
- Catalog/UI: Collection of houseplants to render as cards with care level, light needs, watering frequency, and price
- Image gen: Visual of healthy plant appearance, foliage variations, or care setup
- Sandbox: Calculate watering and fertilizer schedules based on room temperature and humidity

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for watering calculation, Cloud Trace for latency tracking
